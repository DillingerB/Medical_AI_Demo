About:

What this app does for you, the user:

Granted this came form a project, but this is supposed to help you be less risky with certain medications, prevent OD, and other means of something bad happening with your prescriptions. We want to prevent any harm, and have the causes lessen in this sense. So our AI will help prevent this, sending alert notifications to you incase something harmful pops up in it's database, could be taking too much of the medication, could be mixing prescriptions that could end up being dangerous. It's here to help YOU the reader.
